STRATEGIC CASE STUDY · FRAGRANCE & E-COMMERCE

Majestic
Roobee

A digital marketing strategy designed to increase product discovery, online purchases, repeat customers, and awareness for a Nigerian fragrance and body-care brand.

Visit Brand Website ↗
01

THE BUSINESS

Fragrance as identity, confidence and self-expression.

Majestic Roobee is a Nigerian fragrance and body-care brand offering perfumes, perfume oils, body mists, deodorants, feminine-care products and related fragrance products.

The brand positions fragrance as more than a product. Its messaging connects scent with confidence, individuality and personal expression.

02

OPPORTUNITY

Turn product variety into easier buying decisions.

With a broad product catalogue, the marketing challenge is not simply generating attention. The opportunity is helping customers discover the right product, understand its value, and move confidently toward purchase.

BUSINESS

Fragrance

MODEL

E-Commerce

CORE ACTION

Purchase

03

MARKETING OBJECTIVES

Build demand, then convert it.

01

Increase qualified traffic to the online store.

02

Increase product discovery and engagement.

03

Convert high-intent visitors into customers.

04

Build a retargeting system for non-buyers.

05

Encourage repeat purchases and customer value.

04

AUDIENCE STRATEGY

Different customers need different reasons to buy.

01

New Customers

People interested in perfumes, beauty, personal care, fashion and lifestyle.

02

Engaged Users

People who interact with content, advertisements, social profiles or products.

03

High Intent

Website visitors, product viewers, cart users and previous customers.

05

META ADS STRATEGY

Make the fragrance impossible to ignore.

Meta would be used as a major discovery and conversion channel because fragrance is highly visual and lends itself well to lifestyle-led creative.

PRODUCT

Showcase individual fragrances, bundles and best-selling products.

LIFESTYLE

Connect fragrances with confidence, occasions, identity and personal style.

SOCIAL PROOF

Use genuine customer reviews and product experiences to reduce purchase hesitation.

OFFERS

Promote relevant bundles, promotions and seasonal purchasing opportunities.

06

GOOGLE ADS

Capture customers already searching.

Google Search can complement Meta by capturing people actively looking for perfumes, fragrance products, specific scent categories and related products.

SEARCH

High-intent keywords

SHOPPING

Product discovery

REMARKETING

Re-engage visitors

07

CONVERSION FUNNEL

From first impression to repeat customer.

01

Discover

Customer discovers the brand through advertising, search or content.

02

Explore

Customer views products, categories, reviews and offers.

03

Purchase

Customer completes the purchase through the online store.

04

Retarget

Non-buyers receive relevant follow-up messaging based on their behaviour.

05

Retain

Previous customers receive relevant product and promotional communication.

08

EXPERIENTIAL MARKETING

Take the brand beyond the screen.

Majestic Roobee's experiential perfume studio in Abuja creates an opportunity to connect digital marketing with a physical customer experience.

01

Use Meta campaigns to promote perfume-making experiences.

02

Target Abuja audiences interested in beauty, fragrance and lifestyle experiences.

03

Retarget people who engage with studio content but do not book.

04

Turn customer experiences into authentic social content.

DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

E-commerce should make discovery feel effortless.

The strategy connects advertising, product discovery, website experience, conversion, and retargeting into one customer journey.
